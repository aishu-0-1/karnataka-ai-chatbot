
TOPIC: HISTORY AND HERITAGE OF KARNATAKA

Karnataka has a rich history dating back to ancient times.

The Kadamba dynasty was one of the earliest kingdoms
to establish Kannada as an administrative language.

The Western Ganga dynasty ruled parts of southern Karnataka.

The Chalukyas of Badami were famous for their temples
and rock-cut architecture.

The Rashtrakuta dynasty was known for its powerful rule,
literature, art and architecture.

The Hoysala dynasty built magnificent temples at Belur
and Halebidu with detailed stone carvings.

The Vijayanagara Empire made Hampi a major centre of
trade, culture, architecture and political power.

The Kingdom of Mysore, ruled by the Wodeyars,
contributed to education, music, culture and development.

Tipu Sultan was a ruler of Mysore known for his resistance
against British expansion.

Karnataka became part of the state reorganisation in 1956.
Mysore State was renamed Karnataka in 1973.

Famous historical places include Hampi, Badami, Aihole,
Pattadakal, Belur, Halebidu and Srirangapatna.
