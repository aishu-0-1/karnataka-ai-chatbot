
TOPIC: KARNATAKA CULTURE AND TOURISM

Karnataka is famous for its rich culture, traditions,
heritage, art and tourism.

Kannada is the official language of Karnataka.
Kannada literature has a long and important history.

Mysore Dasara is one of the famous festivals of Karnataka.
It is celebrated with cultural programs and a grand procession.

Ugadi, Makara Sankranti, Deepavali and Ganesh Chaturthi
are also celebrated across the state.

Yakshagana is a traditional theatre and dance form
popular in coastal Karnataka.

Dollu Kunitha is a traditional drum dance.
Karnataka is also known for classical music and folk arts.

Famous Karnataka foods include Mysore Masala Dosa,
Bisi Bele Bath, Ragi Mudde, Neer Dosa,
Mysore Pak and Dharwad Peda.

Famous tourist places include Mysore Palace, Hampi,
Coorg, Gokarna, Chikmagalur, Jog Falls,
Belur, Halebidu, Badami and Udupi.

Hampi is known for its Vijayanagara Empire ruins
and historical monuments.

Coorg is famous for coffee plantations, green hills
and natural scenery.

Gokarna is known for its beaches and temples.

Karnataka tourism includes heritage sites, hill stations,
waterfalls, wildlife reserves, beaches and temples.
