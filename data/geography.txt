
TOPIC: GEOGRAPHY AND NATURAL RESOURCES OF KARNATAKA

DISTRICT: BAGALKOTE
1. Bagalkote is located in northern Karnataka.
2. The Krishna River and its tributaries are important to the region.
3. The district has dry landscapes, plains and rocky areas.
4. Agriculture is supported by irrigation and fertile river-basin areas.

DISTRICT: BALLARI
1. Ballari is located in the eastern part of Karnataka.
2. The district has rocky hills and dry plains.
3. The region has mineral resources, including iron ore.
4. The climate is generally hot and dry for much of the year.

DISTRICT: BELAGAVI
1. Belagavi is located in northwestern Karnataka.
2. The district has plains, hills and forested areas.
3. The Krishna River basin is important to parts of the district.
4. The Western Ghats influence rainfall in its western areas.

DISTRICT: BENGALURU RURAL
1. Bengaluru Rural surrounds parts of the Bengaluru metropolitan region.
2. The district has plains, rocky landscapes and agricultural land.
3. Groundwater and local reservoirs support water needs.
4. The region has a mix of rural landscapes and developing settlements.

DISTRICT: BENGALURU URBAN
1. Bengaluru Urban is located in southeastern Karnataka.
2. The district lies on an elevated plateau of the Deccan region.
3. Lakes and reservoirs are important local water resources.
4. The region has a mild climate compared with many lowland areas.

DISTRICT: BENGALURU SOUTH
