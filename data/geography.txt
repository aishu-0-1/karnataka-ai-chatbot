
TOPIC: GEOGRAPHY AND NATURAL RESOURCES OF KARNATAKA

Karnataka is located in southwestern India.
Its capital city is Bengaluru.

Karnataka has 31 districts.

The state shares borders with Goa, Maharashtra,
Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Kerala.

The major geographical regions include the Coastal Karnataka,
Western Ghats, Malnad and Deccan Plateau.

Major rivers include Cauvery, Krishna, Tungabhadra,
Sharavathi, Kali and Kabini.

The Western Ghats are famous for forests, mountains,
waterfalls and biodiversity.

Jog Falls is a famous waterfall on the Sharavathi River.

Karnataka has a tropical climate.
The coastal region receives heavy monsoon rainfall,
while inland regions have different rainfall patterns.

Major mineral resources include iron ore, gold,
limestone, manganese and granite.

The state is also known for forests, wildlife,
agricultural land and water resources.
